# Awesome-Research-Administration

## Top Research Administration Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Grants Management, Pre-Award/Post-Award, IRB/Compliance, Proposal Tracking, Research Information Systems & eRA*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Research Administration**. These systems support universities, research institutes, and funding bodies with proposal development, grant lifecycle management, IRB/ethics review, conflict-of-interest disclosure, award tracking, and research information management.



**Examples** include InfoEd Global, Cayuse, Streamlyne Research, Pivot-RP, Huron Research Suite, IRBNet, eRA Commons, Novelution, OpenWater, and Symplectic Elements (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full enterprise research administration suites are almost entirely commercial. Useful open options exist for research data management, clinical research admin prototypes, grant tracking, and IRB workflows. This section lists the strongest available projects and is realistic about the significant gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[InfoEd Global](https://www.infoedglobal.com/)**  

  Comprehensive electronic research administration (eRA) platform covering proposal development, award management, compliance, and sponsored programs.



- **[Cayuse](https://cayuse.com/)**  

  Widely adopted research administration suite with strong pre-award, proposal routing, IRB, and compliance modules for higher education.



- **[Streamlyne Research](https://www.streamlyne.com/)**  

  Research administration platform supporting pre- and post-award processes, often noted for configurability and institutional deployments.



- **[Pivot-RP](https://pivot.proquest.com/)**  

  Funding opportunity and researcher profile platform (Clarivate/ProQuest) used for finding grants and connecting researchers to opportunities.



- **[Huron Research Suite](https://www.huronconsultinggroup.com/)**  

  Enterprise research administration and compliance suite covering grants, IRB, COI, and related research operations.



- **[IRBNet](https://www.irbnet.org/)**  

  Electronic IRB and research compliance management platform used by many institutions for protocol submission and review.



- **[eRA Commons](https://www.era.nih.gov/)**  

  U.S. federal (NIH-focused) electronic research administration system for grant application, review, and award management.



- **[Novelution](https://www.novelution.com/)**  

  Research administration and grants management software supporting institutional research offices.



- **[OpenWater](https://www.getopenwater.com/)**  

  Platform for managing awards, grants, scholarships, and application review workflows.



- **[Symplectic Elements](https://www.symplectic.co.uk/)**  

  Research information management system focused on publications, profiles, and institutional research reporting (often paired with broader admin tools).



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[InvenioRDM](https://inveniosoftware.org/products/rdm/)**  

  Open-source research data management repository platform based on Invenio and Zenodo — strong for institutional research data and outputs.



- **[RSpace](https://github.com/rspace-os)**  

  Open-source research platform and electronic lab notebook / research data management system connecting researchers to institutional tools and FAIR workflows.



- **[CLARA (Clinical Research Administration)](https://github.com/bianjiang/clara)**  

  Open-source clinical research administration system developed at UAMS for IRB, budgeting, contracting, and related research admin tasks.



- **[Harbor](https://github.com/okeefedaniel/harbor)**  

  Open-source government / enterprise grants management suite covering opportunity posting, applications, review, awards, and closeout.



- **[IRB Tracker and academic IRB systems](https://github.com/)**  

  Community and university projects for protocol submission, review tracking, and ethics committee workflows.



- **[Kuali Research / historical open Coeus-related efforts](https://github.com/)**  

  Legacy and community discussions around open research administration frameworks (note: current Kuali offerings are primarily commercial).



- **[VIVO and research profiling open systems](https://github.com/)**  

  Open researcher profile and research information tools that complement administrative systems.



- **[Grant application and review open portals](https://github.com/)**  

  Lightweight open frameworks for managing internal funding competitions and peer review.



- **[Document and workflow open engines for compliance](https://github.com/)**  

  General open workflow, form, and document management systems adapted for COI, effort reporting, or protocol routing.



- **[Research data and repository open stacks (DSpace, Dataverse, etc.)](https://github.com/)**  

  Mature open repository platforms often integrated into broader research administration ecosystems.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using **InvenioRDM** or **RSpace** for research data and output management alongside commercial admin systems.

- Exploring **CLARA** or similar clinical/academic projects for IRB and research operations in specialized settings.

- Deploying open grants tools like **Harbor** for internal or government-style funding programs.

- Building lightweight compliance workflows on open form and document platforms for smaller institutions.

- Accepting that full pre-award to post-award enterprise suites with deep federal system integration, complex routing, and audit-ready compliance still require commercial platforms (Cayuse, InfoEd, Huron, Streamlyne, etc.).



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Manage proposals and awards in a structured database or open grants tool → handle IRB/ethics with dedicated open or lightweight trackers → store research outputs in InvenioRDM or similar → report via open BI tools. This can support smaller or specialized research offices. Commercial platforms (Cayuse, InfoEd Global, Huron Research Suite, Streamlyne, IRBNet, Symplectic Elements, etc.) remain the practical choice for research-intensive universities needing integrated, compliant, and supported research administration at scale.
